## About The Company Theatre

**Founded:** 2004  
**Founders:** Allan Hawco and Philip Riccio  
**Artistic Director:** Philip Riccio  
**Location:** Toronto, Canada

**Mission:** The Company Theatre produces provocative international plays with Canada's best actors. They strive to create theatre magic with authentic text-based performances, combining hard-hitting, contemporary texts from around the world with some of Canada's best performers.

**Working Process:** 
- No repetitive choices or oppressive choreography
- Just humans making discoveries right before your eyes
- Every performance is an authentic and entirely unique retelling
- No dedicated venue - rent or partner with other theatres
