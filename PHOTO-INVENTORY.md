# Company Theatre Photo Inventory

This document provides a complete reference for all photos used on The Company Theatre website.
Photos follow a consistent naming convention for easy management and replacement.

## Naming Convention

### Cast Headshots
**Format:** `cast-XXX-firstname-lastname.jpg`
- `XXX`: Three-digit sequential number (001-999)
- All lowercase, hyphen-separated

### Show Photos
**Format:** `show-[showslug]-XXX.jpg`
- `showslug`: Production URL identifier (e.g., `jerusalem`, `things-i-know-to-be-true`)
- `XXX`: Three-digit sequential number per show

### Logos & Brand Assets
**Format:** `logo-[variant].[ext]`
- Variants: `logo-main.png`, `logo-white.png`, `logo-color-large.png`

### Hero/Featured Images
**Format:** `hero-[showslug].[ext]` or `feature-[description].[ext]`
