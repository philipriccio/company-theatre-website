# Jackpot Twins

## Overview
- **Title:** Jackpot Twins (originally "Plinko")
- **Playwright:** Philip Riccio
- **Production:** Company Theatre co-production with Mirvish Productions
- **Season:** Off Mirvish 2026-27 season
- **Announcement Date:** March 31, 2026

## Status
- Website being updated in preparation for announcement
- This is Company Theatre's first production in 3 years (since *Things I Know to be True*, Feb 2023)
- First time Philip's own writing produced under Company Theatre

## Venue
- **CAA Theatre** — ~800 seats, 651 Yonge St, Toronto
- Not to be confused with CAA Ed Mirvish Theatre (different venue)
- Same venue as *Things I Know to be True* (2023)

## About Off Mirvish
- Established Mirvish brand for plays (vs their main season which is mostly musicals/big touring shows)
- Where Mirvish takes their "risks" — more adventurous programming
- Still a commercial run at scale (~800 seats)
- Plays at CAA Theatre
